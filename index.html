About the Foundation

The Khan Bridge Foundation was founded by Soliha Fouzan Khan in 2025 and officially launched in 2026. The organization was created with the vision of promoting humanity, compassion, and unity within society. The foundation believes that meaningful change begins when individuals choose empathy, responsibility, and service toward their communities.

Supported by a small but dedicated team, the foundation is working to grow its network of volunteers and partners who share the same mission of creating hope and opportunity across communities.

Our Vision

Humanity

Encouraging compassion, dignity, and kindness in everyday life.

Community

Building bridges between people, resources, and opportunities.

Hope

Creating a future where every street carries a ray of hope.

Unity

Bringing individuals together to support and uplift one another.

Founder

Soliha Fouzan Khan is the Founder of Khan Bridge Foundation. The organization began with a few committed colleagues and continues to grow with individuals who believe in serving humanity and creating meaningful social impact.
